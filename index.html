Age-Wise Learning

Four segments. One progressive learning journey.

Content complexity increases with age, while unnecessary repetition is avoided.

SEGMENT 01

Classes 1–3

Ages 6–8. Curiosity, observation, imagination, simple real-world awareness and healthy habits.

SEGMENT 02

Classes 4–6

Ages 9–11. Awareness, simple logic, digital literacy, money basics, communication and exploration.

SEGMENT 03

Classes 7–9

Ages 12–14. Analysis, decision-making, AI, finance, entrepreneurship, cybersecurity and real-world problems.

SEGMENT 04

Classes 10–12

Ages 15–17. Strategy, advanced technology, careers, personal finance, startups and future opportunities.
