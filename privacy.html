Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

CikNotebooks ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information submitted through our website, particularly through our school and B2B requirement form.

1. Information We Collect

When a school, institution, organisation or authorised representative submits a requirement through our B2B form, we may collect the following information:

  • School Name
  • Contact Person Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • Classes / Segment
  • Student Count
  • Required Quantity
  • Number of Notebooks per Student
  • Custom Cover requirement
  • Custom Content requirement
  • Preferred Delivery Date
  • Additional Requirements or messages submitted through the form

2. Why We Collect This Information

The information is collected to understand the school's requirements and to communicate with the school regarding CikNotebooks products and B2B requirements.

This may include:

  • Understanding notebook quantity requirements
  • Preparing and communicating quotations
  • Discussing customization requirements
  • Planning delivery requirements
  • Responding to enquiries
  • Managing school and institutional orders
  • Providing customer support and follow-up communication

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted by you only for legitimate business purposes connected with your enquiry, quotation, customization, order, delivery or communication with CikNotebooks.

4. Data Sharing

CikNotebooks does not sell or rent submitted personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, process an order, complete delivery, operate the website or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain submitted information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including enquiry handling, quotation, order management, customer support, record keeping and applicable legal or business requirements.

7. Your Choices

If you have submitted information to CikNotebooks and have questions about its use, correction or deletion, you may contact us using the email address provided below.

8. Children's Information

The CikNotebooks B2B requirement form is intended for schools, institutions and their authorised representatives. It is not intended to directly collect personal information from students through the B2B requirement form.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party hosting, form-processing, analytics, communication or other technical services. Where such services are used, information may be processed by those providers as necessary for the operation of the website or requested services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
